# Competitor snapshot — researched 2026-10-04

This is a product-direction snapshot, not a legal claim that features are exclusive.

| Product | Main strength | Representative features | Public price signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Right Inbox | Gmail productivity extension | send later, recurring mail, reminders, notes, templates, signatures, tracking, sequences, CRM, mail merge | free limited; Professional listed at $9.95 monthly / $7.95 annualized; Team $19.95 / $16.95 |
| Boomerang | Gmail scheduling/follow-up | send later, reminders, Inbox Pause, tracking, recurring, meeting tools | free limited; Personal $4.98/mo annual billing; Pro $14.98; Premium $49.98 |
| Mailbutler | productivity + collaboration | tracking, templates, mail merge, smart follow-ups, team assignment | free starter; paid tiers |
| SaneBox | inbox filtering/cleanup | SaneLater, Screener, no-reply, reminders, cleanup | starts around $4.54/mo on bi-yearly plan |
| Clean Email | cleanup | smart folders, cleaning suggestions, unsubscribe, bulk actions, automation | paid consumer cleanup product |
| Shortwave | modern client + AI | AI filters, bundles, split inbox, todos, snooze, unsubscribe, privacy image proxy, tracking | freemium/client model |
| Superhuman Mail | premium productivity client | AI, collaboration, calendar, CRM, advanced team features | $25/user/mo annual Starter; $33 Business |

## Product gap to pursue

Do **not** compete by cloning one competitor feature-for-feature.

The stronger position is the intersection:

**open source + local-first + unified multi-provider client + strong mail security + reversible cleanup +
productivity + transparent risk explanations.**

That is materially different from “Right Inbox but cheaper”.
