# Founding Partner Program

## Purpose

Convert early interest into paid co-development without pretending the Alpha is a finished security
product.

## Offer 1 — Design Partner

**Starting at €4,900**

Best for an organization that wants its use case represented before stable release.

Deliverables can include:
- one requirements/threat-model workshop;
- provider/environment assessment;
- prioritized requirements document;
- evaluation build(s);
- direct maintainer review session;
- end-of-engagement technical memo.

Recommended term: 4–6 weeks.

## Offer 2 — Pilot Partner

**Starting at €12,500**

Best for a team preparing a controlled evaluation.

Deliverables can include:
- deployment plan;
- provider integration work within agreed scope;
- controlled pilot onboarding;
- policy/rule configuration;
- priority issue triage;
- pilot report;
- stable-release roadmap for the customer.

Recommended term: 90 days.

## Offer 3 — Strategic / OEM Partner

**Custom; five-figure annual minimum expected**

Best for:
- MSP/security vendor;
- appliance/vendor integration;
- white-label distribution;
- commercial license requirements;
- large-scale deployment;
- sponsored roadmap development.

## Qualification questions

Before quoting:
1. How many users/mailboxes?
2. Gmail, Microsoft 365, IMAP or mixed?
3. What problem is expensive today?
4. Is this evaluation, internal production, redistribution or OEM?
5. Required timeline?
6. Required support/SLA?
7. Security/compliance requirements?
8. What would a successful pilot save or prevent financially?

Price against business value and delivery risk, not against the cost of an ordinary browser
extension.
