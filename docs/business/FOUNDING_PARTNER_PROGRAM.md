# Founding Partner Program

## Purpose
Convert early interest into paid co-development without presenting the Alpha as a finished security
product.

## Design Partner
Best for organizations that want their environment and use case represented before stable release.

Possible deliverables:
- requirements/threat-model workshop;
- provider/environment assessment;
- prioritized requirements document;
- evaluation builds;
- direct maintainer review;
- technical memo.

## Pilot Partner
Best for a team preparing a controlled evaluation.

Possible deliverables:
- deployment plan;
- provider integration work within agreed scope;
- controlled pilot onboarding;
- policy/rule configuration;
- priority issue triage;
- pilot report;
- stable-release roadmap.

## Strategic / OEM Partner
Best for MSP/security vendors, appliance/vendor integration, white-label distribution, commercial
license requirements, larger deployments and sponsored roadmap development.

## Qualification
Before quoting, determine mailbox count, provider mix, expensive current problem, intended use
(evaluation/production/redistribution/OEM), timeline, required support and security/compliance
constraints.

Commercial terms are offered individually rather than through a public price list.
