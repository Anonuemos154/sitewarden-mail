# Sales playbook

## Primary message

SiteWarden Mail is not positioned as a cheaper Gmail extension. It is an open, local-first mail
security and operations layer designed to reduce trust in active email content while keeping
mailbox control on the user's device.

## Best early buyers

Prioritize organizations where one successful mailbox incident is worth far more than the pilot:
- MSP / MSSP;
- legal;
- finance/accounting;
- executive offices;
- professional services;
- privacy-sensitive engineering companies;
- organizations with repeated BEC/phishing exposure.

## First call outcome

Do not demo every feature. Establish:
- current provider;
- mail/security stack;
- recent pain/incidents;
- number of protected users;
- who owns the budget;
- desired pilot date;
- integration constraints.

Then choose:
- paid Design Partner;
- paid Pilot Partner;
- ordinary future Community user;
- OEM/commercial-license discussion.

## Price discipline

- Do not discount before reducing scope.
- Do not promise custom development for a small subscription.
- Do not include indefinite support in a one-time pilot fee.
- Quote annual enterprise support separately.
- Charge more for custom providers, regulated environments, onsite work and hard deadlines.

## Expansion path

Design Partner -> Pilot -> annual support/managed policy -> additional seats -> enterprise controls
-> OEM/commercial licensing where relevant.
