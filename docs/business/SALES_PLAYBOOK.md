# Sales playbook

## Primary message
SiteWarden Mail is not positioned as a cheaper Gmail extension. It is an open, local-first mail
security and operations layer designed to reduce trust in active email content while keeping
mailbox control on the user's device.

## Best early buyers
Prioritize MSP/MSSP, legal, finance/accounting, executive offices, professional services,
privacy-sensitive engineering companies and organizations with repeated BEC/phishing exposure.

## First call outcome
Establish current provider, security stack, recent pain/incidents, protected users, budget owner,
desired pilot date and integration constraints.

Then choose:
- Design Partner;
- Pilot Partner;
- future Community user;
- OEM/commercial-license discussion.

## Price discipline
Do not publish a public rate card for high-value security work. Reduce scope before discounting.
Do not promise custom development for a small subscription, and do not include indefinite support
inside a one-time engagement.

## Expansion path
Design Partner -> Pilot -> annual support/managed policy -> additional seats -> enterprise controls
-> OEM/commercial licensing where relevant.
