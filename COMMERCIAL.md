# Commercial use, pilots and enterprise partnerships

SiteWarden Mail is open source under **AGPL-3.0-or-later**. The Community edition is intended to
remain free to use. Commercial revenue is built around deployment, support, managed capabilities,
sponsored development and alternative commercial licensing.

> **Current status: Alpha / Active Development.** Commercial engagements during Alpha are
> evaluation, co-development or consulting engagements. SiteWarden Mail is not yet sold as a
> production security control with a production SLA.

## Ways to work with us

### Founding Sponsor

For organizations that want to fund open email-security infrastructure without requiring a private
fork.

Suggested sponsorship levels:

- Supporter: from €25/month
- Project Sponsor: from €100/month
- Founding Sponsor: from €500/month
- Strategic Sponsor: custom annual agreement

Possible benefits include public recognition, roadmap briefings and early-access previews. Security
features in the Community edition are not intentionally withheld to force sponsorship.

### Design Partner — from €4,900

A fixed-scope engagement for organizations that want to help shape the product before stable
release.

Typical scope:

- requirements and threat-model workshop;
- mailbox/provider architecture review;
- evaluation build access;
- prioritized integration feedback;
- documented findings and implementation recommendations;
- direct maintainer channel during the engagement.

Design Partner work is pre-production unless separately agreed in writing.

### Pilot Partner — from €12,500

A structured 90-day evaluation for teams that want SiteWarden Mail tested against real operational
requirements.

Typical scope:

- technical onboarding;
- provider and deployment planning;
- controlled evaluation deployment;
- policy/rule configuration;
- priority defect triage;
- architecture and security review;
- pilot close-out report and roadmap.

The exact scope, user count and production suitability are defined in the written offer.

### Enterprise / OEM / commercial licensing

Organizations that need one or more of the following should request a commercial agreement:

- embedding or redistributing SiteWarden Mail under terms incompatible with AGPL;
- private OEM/white-label distribution;
- centralized organization policy and fleet management;
- enterprise SSO/SCIM/SIEM integrations;
- managed rules/threat intelligence;
- priority support or contractual SLA;
- custom deployment or regulated-environment integration.

Commercial licensing is **quote-based**. As a planning anchor, commercial embedding/OEM agreements
are expected to start in the **five-figure annual range** and scale with distribution, support and
integration obligations.
